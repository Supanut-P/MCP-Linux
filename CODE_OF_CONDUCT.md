# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in baitonghub-linux-mcp a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or role in the project.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific when discussing code or design.
- Assume good intent while still addressing technical and safety concerns directly.
- Accept feedback gracefully and keep disagreements focused on the work.
- Respect privacy, credentials, logs, and other potentially sensitive operational data.
- Help keep issues and pull requests reproducible and useful to future readers.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Knowingly posting secrets, credentials, private logs, or exploit details that put users at risk.
- Repeatedly ignoring maintainer requests intended to keep collaboration safe and productive.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and to public interactions where someone is representing the baitonghub-linux-mcp project or community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit, hide, reject, lock, or remove contributions that violate this Code of Conduct and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the repository maintainers. Do not include security vulnerabilities, credentials, or sensitive personal information in a public issue.
